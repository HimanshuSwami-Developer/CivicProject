About Sankalp Adhikar Vishwas Ekta Party

We stand for a nation where every voice is heard, every community thrives, and our shared values define our path forward. This is who we are, and what we're building together.

Our Leadership

Chairman James Sterling

Chairman Sterling founded the party on a simple belief: that a nation succeeds when its people are heard, its institutions are transparent, and its opportunities are shared by all. Under his leadership, the party has grown into a movement grounded in integrity, unity, and measurable results for the communities it serves.

Every policy we champion and every rupee we raise is guided by that same commitment — leading with vision, uniting with purpose.
